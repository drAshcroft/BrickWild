# Stylized Nature MegaKit

Quaternius "Stylized Nature MegaKit" (Standard licence, see
`License_Standard.txt`), copied from
`C:/Projects/itch_assets/quaternius/Stylized Nature MegaKit[Standard]/glTF`.

68 plants and ground pieces — the common trees, pines and twisted trees, five
dead trees, ferns and plants, clover, the flowers, four grasses, mushrooms,
medium rocks, pebbles, petals and the round and square stepping stones. Every
`.gltf` is prefixed `Wild_` on disk so its `DeadTree_1` and the Nature Kit's do
not collide as catalogue keys; the `.bin` buffers keep their own names.

Measured like every other pack, with a `canopy` and a `trunk` radius as well
as a bounding box — see `assets/props/nature/README.md` for why.
