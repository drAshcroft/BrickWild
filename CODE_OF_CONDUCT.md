# Code of conduct

Be respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests, and project spaces.
Discuss the work and its evidence. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate
against, or disclose private information about other participants.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and restrict participation to
keep the project usable. Report conduct concerns privately to a maintainer
through an available private repository contact. Do not post another person's
private information in an issue.
