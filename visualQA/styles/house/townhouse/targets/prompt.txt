Use case: stylized-concept. Landscape four-panel practical reference board for a procedural TOWNHOUSE in a fantasy Godot game. Labels: 'TOWNHOUSE — STREET', 'ENTRY / STAIR', 'PARLOUR / KITCHEN', 'UPPER BEDROOM'. Same narrow three-level inhabited house throughout, a believable restrained stylized 3D environment with buildable structure and human scale. Distinct urban architecture: narrow frontage, articulated floor bands, grouped bays, modest projecting upper timber storey over masonry ground floor, steep tiled roof between neighbouring houses, sheltered street threshold, useful daylight. Exterior must show a home with a coherent vertical hierarchy. Entry/stair eye-level: compact entrance, coat hooks and small bench, comfortable continuous stair with credible steps, landing, railing, clear headroom and daylight; no enormous empty hall, tight spiral or doors hitting stairs. Parlour/kitchen: intimate fitted cooking wall and hearth, small dining group near a window, modest domestic seating and a useful book shelf, clear route to stairs. Upper bedroom: privacy behind a door, modest bed, bedside surface, wardrobe/chest and curtains, quiet wall textile. Show layered public-to-private spaces, deliberately grouped furniture, supported roof and floors. Warm wood, worn plaster, stone, subdued textiles. Half the visible floor remains clear; sparse shelves and tabletops, no clutter piles, huge furniture, excessive ornaments, people or watermarks. Whole ceiling present in interior views, natural daylight, calm domestic mood. Concept art is a design target, not proof of engineering or code compliance.
