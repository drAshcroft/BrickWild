Create one landscape architectural concept-reference sheet for a compact, buildable circular temple of the Crimson Choir. Use a clean three-panel composition with no words, labels, captions, symbols, watermark, border, or UI: (1) a three-quarter exterior hero showing the whole silhouette; (2) a roof-open cutaway from above, showing the complete circular room and route; (3) a human-eye interior view from the entrance aimed along the ritual axis. Keep all three panels faithful to the same building, dimensions, materials and openings. The exterior should occupy about half the sheet; reserve enough space for the cutaway and eye-level room to read clearly.

Architecture: a grounded, thick-walled cylindrical stone tholos, low and broad rather than a tower; a clear single entrance with a short paved threshold; a visible circular colonnade inside the drum; a restrained ring roof or shallow circular crown supported by that colonnade, with a deliberate central oculus that does not close the central void. Show the circular ambulatory as a continuous walkable band. A single straight, substantial stone bridge crosses the central chasm on the entrance axis. At its far end, a small raised dais carries an altar and one upright, human-scale ritual figure. The sequence must read at a glance: doorway → clear approach → bridge over open pit → altar → figure. Keep the pit visibly open beside the bridge; do not put the altar or columns across the bridge route.

Blood-cult expression: warm, muted crimson in a few stone inlays and altar details, with several carefully spaced ritual bowls or wall flames along the perimeter and a small number of hanging chains near the focus. Show traces of repeated ceremony as deliberate marks at the altar, not gore or surface-wide red staining. Use pale weathered limestone and dark iron accents, restrained warm ember light, and enough indirect daylight through the oculus for the columns, floor ring and bridge to remain legible.

Use believable classical masonry proportions and simple repeated structural members with clear contact and support. Leave broad stretches of the floor and wall visually quiet. The atmosphere is solemn and communal, not a dungeon, arena, generic round tower, or prop-filled fantasy room. No detached roof ornament; every visible member belongs to the wall, ring, colonnade, bridge or altar structure. High-quality game-environment concept art, natural material response, readable architectural geometry, moderate detail, neutral ground and sky.

Avoid: a sealed solid dome over the void; a completely empty black hole with no bridge; a tiny bridge or altar hidden behind the pit; an altar placed on the threshold; floating columns; a tall needle spire; rows of unrelated furniture; piles of skulls; blood splatter; exaggerated gore; dense chains; random clutter; unreadable darkness; impossible multi-level circulation; text or labels.
