Create one landscape architectural concept-reference sheet for a monumental but walkable Bloodpit Basilica, the Crimson Choir's corrupted cathedral-plan temple. Use a clean three-panel composition with no words, labels, captions, symbols, watermark, border, or UI: (1) a three-quarter exterior hero showing the whole building and its approach; (2) a roof-open cutaway from above showing nave, side aisles, apse and route; (3) a human-eye view from the west entrance looking straight down the nave to the ritual focus. Keep all panels faithful to the same architecture and scale. Give the exterior a little over half the sheet; make the interior plan and eye-level aisle clear enough to inspect.

Architecture: a long rectangular basilica with a readable west-front entrance and a shallow, projecting pronaos; continuous paving connects the forecourt through the real gate. A tall central nave rises above two lower side aisles. The nave's low-pitched gable and raised clerestory are borne by a measured rhythm of substantial inner columns and longitudinal beams; the aisle roofs remain below the clerestory openings so real upper light reaches the nave. Repeated bays lead toward a semicircular apse at the far end. A small number of side cells open from the aisles with visible thresholds; keep them subordinate to the main hall. The altar and one upright ritual figure stand in the apse, aligned after the altar on the central axis. A restrained, supported spire or lantern rises over that sanctum volume, clearly part of the roof hierarchy rather than an isolated tower. The exterior must read as a long church hall with a raised nave, lower shoulders, portico and sanctum marker, not as a featureless barn box.

Blood-cult expression: muted crimson stone bands or inlays focus on the west threshold, altar and a few bay markers. Place a limited, orderly number of braziers or wall flames along the side aisles, leaving a broad, straight, unobstructed central procession. Add a few chains near the sanctum and subtle signs of repeated communal rite at the altar. No gore, red flood, or prop clutter. Use pale, weathered masonry, dark timber roof structure, and controlled warm light that makes the apse, colonnade, clerestory and aisle openings readable without turning the whole interior bright red.

Show believable load paths: columns visibly meet beams, the raised nave bears on its inner colonnade, lower aisle roofs meet outer walls, and the pronaos has supported columns and a clear door approach. Preserve generous visible floor in the center aisle and practical walking width around the altar. Sparse, plausible furnishings only. High-quality game-environment concept art, clear architectural hierarchy, natural stone and timber, moderate ornament, grounded perspective.

Avoid: one flat roof over the whole rectangle; a generic barn silhouette; columns that stop short of or pierce their beams; blocked clerestory windows; a rear spire unrelated to the apse; a forest of columns hiding the route; braziers in the center aisle; inaccessible side cells; repeated furniture filling empty floor; a tiny distant idol lost in darkness; exaggerated gore; text or labels.
