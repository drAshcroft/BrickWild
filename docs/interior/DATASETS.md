# Interior layout datasets: survey, downloads, first statistics

Written 2026-11-14 for the complaint that generated interiors "lack legitimacy".
Data only; the written design guides are a separate task. All downloads sit
under `artifacts/`, which `.gitignore` excludes (`artifacts/*`, line 7;
checked with `git check-ignore`). Nothing here will be committed.

## Verdict in one paragraph

No single free dataset has real houses with room types, walls and movable
furniture together. Three free ones cover it between them.

* **HSSD-hab** covers furniture placed in whole houses. The houses are human-authored and the data is ungated.
* **SUN RGB-D** covers furniture in real rooms, measured from scans. The views are partial.
* **Swiss Dwellings** covers how real apartments are planned: room sizes, door graphs, windows, fixed fittings. It has no movable furniture.

The 1709 De Graeff inventory is the one free, structured, room-by-room historic
source found. The strongest furniture datasets, 3D-FRONT, Structured3D,
Matterport3D, ScanNet and ZInD, sit behind agreement forms. The user must
apply for those; see "Gated" below.

## How to load it

Scripts are in `C:\Projects\BrickWild\artifacts\interior_research\scripts\`.
Run them with `python -I`, never from inside a data dir.

```
python -I scripts/stats_hssd.py    artifacts/interior_data/hssd_hab
python -I scripts/stats_sunrgbd.py artifacts/interior_data/sunrgbd
python -I scripts/stats_swiss.py   artifacts/interior_data/swiss_dwellings/swiss-dwellings-v3.0.0/geometries.csv 4
python -I scripts/stats_degraeff.py artifacts/interior_data/degraeff_1709/Transcription_DeGraeff_inventory_v1.csv
# re-fetch helpers: fetch_hssd.py (anonymous, token=False), remote_zip.py (range-read any zip), fetch_sunrgbd_scenes.py
```

Frame conventions, measured rather than assumed:

* **HSSD.** World y is up and the plan is (x, z). The quaternion is `[w, x, y, z]`. An object's translation is its base centre: a bed GLB spans x -0.89..0.87, y 0..1.2, z -1.15..1.15. The model's **-z end is its back**, the end that meets the wall: wardrobe 0.97, bed 0.90, toilet 0.95, fridge 0.94. That holds even though `object_config.json` says `front: [0,0,-1]`. Rooms are `region_annotations[].poly_loop` (x, z) plus `floor_height`. Categories are `semantics/objects.csv:main_category`, with nightstand, desk and coffee table split out by name.
* **SUN RGB-D.** z is up, the camera is at the origin and the floor is near z = -1.2. Box: the rows of `basis` are the axes, `coeffs` are half-extents, and `orientation` is the facing, which points to the bed's FOOT. The layout comes from `gtCorner3D`: the floor half gives the visible floor polygon, which is closed on the camera side.
* **Swiss Dwellings.** `entity_type` is area, opening, feature or separator. WKT is in metres. A door is a polygon spanning the wall. Rooms are joined when both sit within 0.10 m of the door polygon. `ROOM` is the generic sleeping/other room: 82k of them, against 23k explicit `BEDROOM`s.

* Bed to wardrobe gap: median 1.14 m (n=132).

**Plan graph (Swiss sample, 11,273 apartments, 76,051 interior doors).**

* Where doors lead:
  * corridor to room 23%
  * corridor to bathroom 20%
  * corridor to kitchen 7.5%
  * living-dining to balcony 7.3%
  * corridor to bedroom 6.4%
  * kitchen to living-dining 1.8%
  * room to room 0.8%
* The entrance door opens into a corridor in 87% of apartments.
* Doors per room, median: corridor 5, living-dining 2, living room 2, bedroom 1, bathroom 1 (bathroom has exactly 1 in 95% of cases).
* 23% of kitchens have no door: these are open kitchens, drawn as a separate KITCHEN area.
* Windows: bedroom 0.95 and ROOM 0.92 have one or more. So do living-dining 0.90 and kitchen 0.68. Only 0.25 of bathrooms have one, and 0.07 of corridors.
* **Doors sit at corners.** The median distance from a door's edge to the nearest room-polygon vertex is 0.13 m for a ROOM (n=28,907) and 0.09 m for a bathroom. 65-83% of doors are within 0.30 m of a corner.
* Fittings: toilet and bath are 100% in the bathroom, and 82% / 99% of them touch a wall. Kitchen fittings touch a wall 98% of the time. The washing machine is in the bathroom 51% of the time and the storeroom 45%.

**Historic (De Graeff, Amsterdam 1709).** The inventory lists 34 rooms in the
notary's walking order, 1,523 entries in all. Chairs are kept in sets and
outnumber tables about 3 to 1:

* Groote Tapijtse Kamer: 15 chairs, 4 tables
* Kleijne Zijdelkamer: 12 chairs, 1 table
* Slaapkamer Agneta: 14 chairs, 1 bed

Beds turn up outside bedrooms: in the eetzaal (dining hall), the groote kamer,
the attic rooms and the servant's kamertje. The kitchen lists no furniture
entries at all; its 20 entries are vessels and tools. Storage, meaning chests,
cupboards and cabinets, is everywhere.

## Caveats

* HSSD room polygons include open-plan splits that are not walls, so "at wall" is generous for living and dining rooms. 612 furniture objects fell outside every polygon and were skipped.
* HSSD door objects are mostly exterior doors, so its door-adjacency table is exterior-biased and should not be used for the plan graph.
* SUN RGB-D layouts are single-view, so wall-contact and nightstand counts are lower bounds. The set is also heavy with offices and classrooms; the SUN wall-contact table covers every scene type; only chairs-per-table and sofa-to-table are filtered to home scene types.
* All three furniture-placement sources are modern (2000s-2020s). For pre-modern houses, read them as evidence about ergonomics and walls, not period taste. The De Graeff inventory and its siblings (Gunston Hall, Berkshire, Williamsburg) are the period evidence for what a room held.

## Gated: what the user must do

* **3D-FRONT.** Apply on Alibaba Tianchi (dataset 65347, "3D-FRONT"). You need an Aliyun account and must agree to the research terms. The download links arrive by email.
* **Structured3D.** Fill the agreement Google form linked from structured3d-dataset.org. Links follow by email.
* **Matterport3D.** Sign the Terms of Use PDF on niessner.github.io/Matterport and email it to the address there. Ask for `house_segmentations` only, which holds room regions plus object instances.
* **ScanNet.** Sign the ScanNet ToS (scan-net.org) and email it. Scan2CAD poses then need their own form.
* **Zillow ZInD.** Request access through the Bridge Interactive data platform linked from github.com/zillow/zind.
* **Kaggle MSD.** A Kaggle login only. It is redundant with Swiss Dwellings.
